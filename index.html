About Us

YemaKal Golden Bites was created by ten Senior High School student entrepreneurs who wanted to introduce a unique and affordable Filipino snack.

Our product combines two Filipino favorites: yema and binangkal. We use ingredients from local markets to provide delicious and quality snacks at an affordable price.
