Our story

A familiar favorite,
with a sweet twist.

YemaKal was created by Ten Senior High School student entrepreneurs. We brought together two Filipino favorites — yema and binangkal — to make one fun and delicious snack.

We use ingredients from local markets and aim to share a quality, affordable treat with our school and community.
