Our Story

YemaKal was created by ten Senior High School student entrepreneurs in Valencia City, Bukidnon. We opened on February 25, 2025.

We combined two Filipino favorites, yema and binangkal, into one snack. Our ingredients come from local markets, and we sell about 100 pieces a day at Valencia National High School.
