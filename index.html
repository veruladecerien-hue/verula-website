Our story

Made with heart, shared with joy

YemaKal Golden Bites was created by ten Senior High School student entrepreneurs who wanted to introduce a unique and affordable Filipino snack.

The product combines two Filipino favorites: yema and binangkal. We use ingredients from local markets to provide delicious, quality snacks at an affordable price.
